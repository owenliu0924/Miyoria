Scope: the public landing at `/` for a visitor who is not logged in. Visitor mode: Persuade.
Audience: first viewport for her Discord community (meet her, see what she is doing now, log in
with Discord or watch); further down, a section for developers who want to run Miyoria themselves.
Proof: her real state, song and today's numbers from `/api/public/status` and `/api/public/songs`;
the real install commands from the README. Assets: short loops of her recorded from the official
free Live2D model (神宫凉子); only the recordings ship.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Black, with her in it. A real black hole on the cover behind her name, the night sea under
everything after it, and pictures of her doing the things she does instead of a list of features.
Refuses the VTuber template of a glossy standing figure on violet glass, and the SaaS feature grid.

OWN-WORLD: Pure black; white type; light glass (6-8 px blur) only over the water; Geist with the
system Chinese faces, Geist Mono for commands. Colour comes from the shaders and from her.

STORY: A visitor sees the black hole open and her slide in, reads what she is doing now, and logs
in with Discord or goes to watch. Scrolling, the nav folds into a capsule over the sea; she talks
in Discord, sings, streams; a developer reaches 自己養一隻 and copies the commands.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The black hole right of centre, her at right, her live state, name, one sentence
and the two buttons at left.

FORM: Black hole and night sea, chosen by the owner over ten rounds of drafts. seed: none

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
